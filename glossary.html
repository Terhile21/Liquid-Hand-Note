The Glossary

Quick definitions for every term you'll encounter on Liquid — from perp basics to margin mechanics and venue specifics.
⌕
B
Backstop Liquidation
A failsafe mechanism. When a position can't close via normal order book execution, it's transferred to a designated liquidator vault (like HLP). Keeps the system solvent during volatile or illiquid conditions. Some or all maintenance margin may not be returned.
BPS (Basis Points)
1 basis point = 0.01%. Liquid charges 5 bps (0.05%) on Hyperliquid perp trades. Used to express small fee percentages precisely without fractions.
C
Collateral
The funds you post to back a leveraged position. On Liquid (via Hyperliquid), collateral is USDC. What's at risk if the trade moves against you.
Cross Margin
A margin mode where all eligible positions share a single collateral pool. More capital efficient but riskier — a liquidation event in one cross position can affect the collateral available for others.
F
Funding Interval
How often funding payments exchange between longs and shorts — typically every 1 hour or every 8 hours. Longer holds accumulate more funding cost or income.
Funding Rate
A periodic payment between long and short traders to keep perp price aligned with spot. Positive funding = longs pay shorts. Negative = shorts pay longs. The platform collects none of it.
H
HLP
Hyper Liquidity Provider — Hyperliquid's protocol vault. Community-owned, protocol-managed. Provides market-making liquidity, performs liquidations, supplies USDC to Earn, and accrues trading fees. 4-day deposit lock-up. No 10% leader fee.
Hyperliquid
A high-performance Layer-1 blockchain optimised for onchain perpetuals trading. Liquid routes a significant portion of orders through Hyperliquid's HyperCore order book engine.
I
Initial Margin
Collateral required to open a leveraged position. = Position Notional ÷ Leverage. Reserved while your position is open — cannot be freely withdrawn while backing risk.
Isolated Margin
A margin mode where collateral is assigned to a single position only. Liquidation of that position only affects its own collateral — all other positions and balances remain untouched.
L
Leverage
A multiplier on your position size relative to collateral. 10x = you control $10,000 with $1,000. Amplifies gains and losses equally. Liquid supports up to 200x on some assets.
Liquidation
When account equity falls below maintenance margin and the system force-closes your position to prevent further losses. Triggered by mark price, not last traded price.
Long
A trade that profits when the asset price goes up. You go long when you believe an asset will increase in value.
M
Maintenance Margin
The minimum collateral to keep a position open. Lower than initial margin. When equity drops below this level, liquidation is triggered. Varies by asset and leverage tier.
Margin
The collateral you post to open and hold a leveraged position. Think of it as your skin in the game — it's what you risk on each trade.
Mark Price
A fair reference price used for PnL calculation, TP/SL triggers, and liquidation. Derived from oracle data + order book conditions — not just last traded price. Protects against unfair liquidations from momentary wicks.
O
Oracle Price
A fair reference price for an asset derived from external spot markets and institutional data providers (e.g. Pyth). Used to calculate funding rates and mark price. The benchmark the perp is measured against.
Ostium
One of the DEXs Liquid routes orders to. Specialises in real-world assets including FX, commodities, and global equities. Charges fees on entry only — unlike Hyperliquid which charges entry and exit.
P
Perpetual Futures (Perp)
A derivative contract tracking an asset's price with no expiration date. Hold long or short indefinitely with sufficient margin. Anchored to spot via the funding rate mechanism.
PnL
Profit and Loss. Unrealized PnL fluctuates in real time with mark price. Realized PnL locks in when you close. All PnL on Liquid settles in USDC.
Premium Component
Part of the funding rate formula. Measures how far the perp price is trading above or below the oracle/spot price. Positive premium = perp above spot = longs pay shorts.
S
Short
A trade that profits when the asset price goes down. You go short when you believe an asset will decrease in value.
Slippage
The gap between your intended trade price and actual execution price. Large orders move the market against you. TWAP orders minimize slippage by spreading execution over time.
Stop Loss (SL)
An order that auto-closes your position if price moves against you past a set level. Triggered by mark price. Your primary downside protection tool on any leveraged trade.
T
Take Profit (TP)
An order that auto-closes your position when price hits a target in your favour. Triggered by mark price. Locks in gains without manual monitoring.
TWAP
Time-Weighted Average Price. Splits a large order into smaller chunks executed at intervals. Reduces market impact and achieves a better average fill. Orders can be randomised to prevent front-running.
U
Unrealized PnL
Profit or loss on an open position calculated from current mark price. Fluctuates constantly. Not locked in until you close the trade.
V
Vault
An onchain pooled account on Hyperliquid where depositors share exposure to a trading strategy. PnL distributed pro rata. Vault leaders earn 10% of gains (not applicable to HLP). Not a savings product — carries strategy risk.
