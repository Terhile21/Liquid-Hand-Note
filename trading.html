Leverage & Margin Modes

When opening a leveraged position, you pick a margin mode. This determines how collateral is allocated and how liquidation risk spreads.

Cross Margin

  • Shared collateral pool across positions
  • Profits offset losses elsewhere
  • More capital efficient
  • Liquidation can affect other positions

Isolated Margin

  • Collateral locked to one position
  • Liquidation doesn't touch others
  • Tighter per-position risk control
  • Can add margin to reduce liq risk
Initial Margin Required
Initial Margin = Position Notional ÷ Leverage

Higher leverage = smaller margin required = liquidation price closer to entry. Always know your liq price before opening.

TP / SL — Take Profit & Stop Loss

Take Profit (TP)

Closes your position at a target price when market moves in your favour. Locks in gains automatically — no need to watch the screen.

Stop Loss (SL)

Closes your position if price moves against you past a defined level. Your most important risk tool. Always set one. Both TP/SL trigger on mark price.

TWAP Orders

TWAP (Time-Weighted Average Price) breaks a large order into smaller chunks sent at intervals — reducing market impact and getting you a better average fill price.

Low Fees

Some of the lowest taker fees in the market on TWAP execution.

Smart Execution

Orders randomised in time to prevent front-running and manipulation.

Smoother Fills

Avoids large sudden trades that would move the price against you.

Fees by Venue

Liquid routes across three DEXs, each with its own fee structure. Liquid applies 5 bps (0.05%) uniform fee on Hyperliquid perps, and an additional 5 bps on Ostium. No extra fee on Lighter.

Hyperliquid

5 bps on perps. Spot charged at Hyperliquid's own rate. Tiers based on 14-day weighted volume. Maker rebates paid per trade directly to your wallet.

Ostium

Entry only. Crypto 3–10 bps · Stocks/FX/Indices 3–5 bps · Commodities vary. +5 bps Liquid fee on all Ostium trades.

Lighter

Zero additional fees from Liquid — matching Lighter's own zero-fee approach.

Volume Tiers on Hyperliquid

Your fee rate is based on 14-day weighted volume: perp volume + 2× spot volume. Higher volume = lower taker fees. Maker rebates flow directly to your wallet on each trade.
