Liquidations

A liquidation happens when your position moves against you and equity falls below the minimum required margin. The system force-closes your position to prevent further losses.
⚠ Key Risk

Highly leveraged positions hit their liquidation price on very small adverse moves. 10x leverage: ~10% move liquidates you. 50x: ~2%. Always know your liquidation price before opening a position.

Two Margin Thresholds

Initial Margin

The collateral needed to open the position. Reserved while your position is open — cannot be freely withdrawn while it's backing risk.

Maintenance Margin

The minimum collateral to keep the position open. Lower than initial margin. When equity drops below this, liquidation is triggered using the mark price.

The Liquidation Process

STEP 01
Market Liquidation AttemptThe platform submits market orders to the order book to close the position (fully or partially). If enough is closed to restore margin, the position may survive reduced. Any remaining collateral stays with you.
STEP 02
Backstop Liquidation (Failsafe)If the order book can't fill the position — due to thin liquidity or fast-moving markets — the position and its collateral transfer to a designated liquidator vault (like HLP). This keeps the system solvent. Some or all of your maintenance margin may not be returned.

Cross vs Isolated

Cross Margin

Liquidation can affect your full shared collateral pool. A backstop event may transfer your entire cross margin equity. Riskier if over-leveraged across multiple positions simultaneously.

Isolated Margin

Only the isolated position and its specific collateral are affected. Your other positions and cross margin balance remain completely untouched. Better for risk-conscious traders.

Mark Price & Partial Liquidations

Liquidations trigger on mark price — a fair value derived from oracle data and order book conditions. This protects you from unfair liquidations caused by momentary wicks in low-liquidity conditions.

For large positions, partial liquidations may occur first — closing only a portion to restore margin. This reduces market impact and may give you a window to add margin before full liquidation.

How to Protect Yourself

Use Lower Leverage

More distance between entry and liquidation price. Less chance of being stopped out by normal market noise.

Always Set a Stop Loss

Exit on your terms before the liquidation engine does it on its terms. Non-negotiable for leveraged trading.

Monitor Margin Levels

Especially during volatile sessions. Unrealized losses reduce your effective margin in real-time.

Add Margin (Isolated)

If you're on isolated margin and nearing liquidation, you can add more collateral directly to that position.
