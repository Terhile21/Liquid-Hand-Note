What is
Liquid?

Liquid is a non-custodial perpetual futures aggregator — one interface to trade hundreds of markets across multiple DEXs, with up to 200x leverage, 24/7/365.
"If you believe in something, you should make money for being right. Furthermore, you should be able to make more money for being more confident in your prediction, without risking additional dollars." — Franklyn Wang, CEO of Liquid

What You Can Trade

Liquid offers hundreds of markets across every asset class. If it has a price, you can probably trade it here.

Core Features

Non-Custodial

You control your keys. Liquid aggregates execution across DEXs without holding your funds.

Multi-DEX Routing

Orders route across Hyperliquid, Ostium, and Lighter — always seeking best execution.

Mobile-First

Full-featured iOS and Android app. Built for speed, designed for traders on the move.

Easy Onboarding

Email sign-in + Apple Pay deposit. Start trading in minutes — no prior crypto needed.

Up to 200x Leverage

Put up $100, control a $20,000 position. Set leverage freely from 1x to 200x.

Paradigm-Backed

$7.6M seed led by Paradigm, with General Catalyst and Alpen also participating.
